PALS Motor Design Course

Online teaching

Designed and taught an online course that shows engineering students from outside computer science how to combine electromagnetics and electrical engineering with software, using the FEMM simulation tool. Students go from a motor specification to geometry maths, a CAD drawing and a flux simulation. Delivered across India, Bangladesh and Nepal.
